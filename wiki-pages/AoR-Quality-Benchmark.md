# AoR quality benchmark and proposed next evidence

AoR is a design-depth benchmark, not a source of inherited certification. Its public README demonstrates a synthetic control loop and links measured results, installation evidence and detailed trust boundaries. [AoR public reference](https://github.com/jordanmrash/agent-of-record).

## Proposed acceptance criteria

- Complete isolated initialization without test-only schema patches.
- A task-level before/after/control experiment for lesson effectiveness.
- Negative fixtures for receipt tampering, ambiguous retrieval, rollback and path boundaries.
- Independently trusted caller identity and task-outcome evidence.

These are proposed next steps, not capabilities established by the current PR. See [Roadmap](Roadmap) and [Evidence and Limitations](Evidence-and-Limitations).
